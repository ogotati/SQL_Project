# Analytics for Digital Marketing 

<u>Tools</u>: PostgreSQL (DBeaver) for writing SQL queries.

Goal: Explore details of Google and Facebook online campaigns. 

Description: Identify TOP performers based on the following criteria: costs, value, reach, return on marketing investment (ROMI), and company longevity.

Tasks: To process the data in the dataset, I wrote SQL queries using CTE, LEFT JOIN, UNION, CASE, COALESCE, window functions, aggregation functions, grouping, sorting, and working with dates.

Insights: 
Calculated and identified:
- the maximum, minimum, and average for daily expenses; 
- the 5 most effective advertising days, with the highest ROMI; 
- a campaign that achieved record weekly earnings;
- a campaign that had the highest month-to-month increase in reach;
- the advertising campaign with the longest continuous duration.
